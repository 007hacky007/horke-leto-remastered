First public release.

Download the builder for your computer, choose your Horké léto CD or a backup image
of it, and the builder makes a playable game for macOS (Apple silicon) or
Windows 10/11.

- **Horke-Leto-Builder-macOS.dmg**: open it and follow the picture in the window.
  The builder is not signed by Apple, so macOS asks you to allow it once.
- **Horke-Leto-Builder-Windows.exe**: if SmartScreen warns you, choose
  *More info* > *Run anyway*.

The game is not included; you need the original CD. F8 switches between Original
and Remastered graphics, holding Space shows the clickable spots. See the
[README](https://github.com/007hacky007/horke-leto-remastered#readme) for
requirements and answers to common questions.

Found a bug? Open an issue and attach the log from *Zobrazit podrobnosti*.
